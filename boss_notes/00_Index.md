### 🗓️ If you have 7+ days (Complete Mastery Plan)

## 🔮 Exam Prediction (Data-Driven)

Based on statistical analysis of 8 years of exam papers (2017 to 2024) and RUET class lecture notes:

1. **Discrete Mathematics is 50% of the Exam:**
   Since the 2022 curriculum update, Section B is exclusively Discrete Mathematics (4 full questions out of 8, worth 50% of total marks). Mastering Section B is essential for high marks.
2. **DC Circuit Gauss-Seidel Solvers Appear Routinely:**
   In 5 out of 8 papers (2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, and 2024), a DC circuit diagram with mesh currents was given. The governing linear equations must be set up and solved using Gauss-Seidel iterations.
3. **Practical Algorithm Questions Are Rising:**
   In 2024 Q.7(a), a 6-mark question tested binary search, sorting, and greedy delivery routes ([DM-7.1](DM-7_Supplementary/DM-7.1_Algorithms_and_Complexity.md)). Expect similar practical algorithmic applications.
4. **Counting and Pigeonhole Principle Are Heavily Tested:**
   In 2023, Question 8 gave all 10 marks to counting, combinations, binomial theorem, and the pigeonhole principle. Lecture notes strongly emphasize this area.
5. **Infrequent Topics:**
   Optimization ([NM-6](NM-6_Optimization/_Topic_Overview.md)) and Eigenvalues ([NM-9](NM-9_Eigenvalues/_Topic_Overview.md)) have appeared only twice in 8 years. Prepare core definitions, but focus study time on high-frequency numerical methods.
