## 📊 Exam Strategy

### 🗓️ If you have 7+ days (Complete Mastery Plan)

- **Days 1–2 (Section A Core):** Read all 🔴 Tier-1 Numerical Methods files. Focus on:
  * NM-1: Error definitions, propagation formula, Taylor series expansion
  * NM-2: Bisection, False Position, and Newton-Raphson iterations with error bounds
  * NM-3: Gauss-Seidel mesh analysis for DC resistive circuits (NM-3.5, NM-3.8)
  * NM-4: Lagrange interpolation (NM-4.6) and Newton's forward difference (NM-4.7)
  * NM-5: Simpson's 1/3 single and composite applications (NM-5.2)
  * NM-8: Classical RK4 4th-order ODE solver (NM-8.5)
- **Days 3–4 (Section B Core):** Read all 🔴 Tier-1 and top 🟠 Discrete Mathematics files:
  * DM-3: Truth tables, conditional traps, tautology proofs, normal forms (DNF/CNF), rules of inference
  * DM-5: Modular congruence, division algorithm, Fermat's Little Theorem, modular exponentiation
  * DM-6: Pigeonhole Principle (standard and generalized), combinations, binomial coefficients
  * DM-7: Sorting and search complexity hierarchy, algorithm classification
- **Day 5 (Secondary Topics & Meta Review):**
  * Skim 🟠 and 🟡 files: Curve fitting least squares (NM-4.2, NM-4.3), Central difference tables (NM-4 Lower), PDE 5-point stencils (NM-10), Heun/Euler (NM-8 Lower, NM-8.2), Eigenvalues (NM-9)
  * Read [99_Master_Formula_Sheet.md](99_Master_Formula_Sheet.md) top to bottom. Memorize symbol definitions and constraints
- **Day 6 (Active PYQ Simulation):**
  * Pick 2 recent papers (2024 and 2023). Solve the questions without looking at solutions
  * Cross-check your boxed results against the boss notes
- **Day 7 (Weak Spot Polish & Rest):**
  * Re-solve any calculation that gave an error
  * Sleep at least 7 hours before the exam day to prevent arithmetic slips

## 🔮 Exam Prediction (Data-Driven)

Based on the statistical cross-analysis of all 8 years of exam papers and 3 sets of RUET class notes:

1. **Discrete Mathematics is now 50% of the entire exam:**
   Since the 2022 curriculum revision, Section B is exclusively Discrete Mathematics (4 questions out of 8, worth 50% of total marks). Neglecting Section B is impossible if aiming for first class or A+.
2. **Off-Syllabus and Practical Algorithm Questions are Rising:**
   In 2024 Q.7(a), a 6-mark question appeared testing sorting, binary search, and greedy delivery vans (DM-7.1) despite being outside the explicit syllabus text. Expect similar algorithmic applications of discrete structures.
3. **The DC Circuit Numerical Solver is Virtually Guaranteed:**
   In 5 out of 8 papers (2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, and 2024), a DC circuit diagram with mesh currents and independent/dependent sources was given. The governing equations must be derived and solved by Gauss-Seidel.
4. **Counting and Pigeonhole Principle are Heavily Favored:**
   Fahad's lecture note carries an explicit margin annotation: *"Combination Permutation থেকে Exam-এ আসবে!"*. Raidah's notes recommend preparing 1.5 question sets on counting. In 2023, Question 8 dedicated all 10 marks to counting, combinations, binomial theorem, and pigeonhole principle.
5. **Declining / Infrequent Topics:**
   Eigenvalue power method (NM-9) and Optimization golden section search (NM-6) have appeared only twice in 8 years. Prepare definitions, but prioritize interpolation, integration, ODEs, and linear systems.
