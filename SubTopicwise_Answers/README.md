# 📦 Subtopic-wise Answers Archive

This directory preserves the fine-grained, 84-file subtopic-wise answer collection for **ECE 2213 (Numerical Methods & Discrete Mathematics)** at RUET.

---

## 🏛️ Purpose & Relationship to Topic-wise Clusters

Originally, every examined subtopic in ECE 2213 received its own individual file (84 files total per layer). While extremely detailed, having 84 separate files made topic-level revision fragmented. 

To provide the ultimate study experience:
1. **Consolidated Topic-wise Clusters:** In the parent directory (`../topicwise_answers_exam_style/` and `../topicwise_answers_explanation/`), these 84 files have been merged into **14 comprehensive topic-level modules** (e.g. `NM-1_Error_Analysis.md`, `NM-2_Roots_of_Equations.md`).
2. **Granular Subtopic Archive:** This folder (`SubTopicwise_Answers/`) permanently preserves the individual subtopic files for students and reviewers who wish to inspect a specific subtopic in complete isolation.

---

## 🧭 Navigation System (BossNote Style)

Every subtopic note in both subfolders includes **BossNote-style navigation bars** at the very top and bottom:
- **`[🏠 Subtopic Index](00_Index.md)`**: Jumps instantly to the folder's master subtopic index.
- **`[← Prev: ...]`**: Moves directly to the immediately preceding subtopic in syllabus order.
- **`[Next: ... →]`**: Advances directly to the next subtopic in syllabus order.

---

## ⚖️ Register & Style Standards

- **`topicwise_answers_exam_style/`**: Follows strict RUET exam register (`exam_register.md`). One person in the document (the examinee), concise mathematical derivation, step-by-step arithmetic, boxed answers, zero filler.
- **`topicwise_answers_explanation/`**: Follows pedagogical explanation register (`writing_style.md`). Explains *why* formulas work, common pitfalls, and warning callouts (`> [!warning]`). All boxed numbers match the exam-style answers character-for-character.
