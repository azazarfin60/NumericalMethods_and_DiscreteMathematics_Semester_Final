**4.1** Explain the method of least squares to fit a straight line of the form $Y = a_0 + a_1 x$ to the data $(x_i, y_i)$:

| $x$ | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |

| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |

**4.3** If the straight line $Y = a_0 + a_1 x$ is the best fit to the set of data points $(x_1, y_1), (x_2, y_2), \dots, (x_n, y_n)$, show that

$$
\begin{vmatrix}
x & y & 1 \\
\Sigma x_i & \Sigma y_i & n \\
\Sigma x_i^2 & \Sigma x_i y_i & \Sigma x_i
\end{vmatrix} = 0, \quad i = 1, 2, \dots, n.
$$
