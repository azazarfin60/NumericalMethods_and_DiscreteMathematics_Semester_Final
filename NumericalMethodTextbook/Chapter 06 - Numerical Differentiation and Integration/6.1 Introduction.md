## 6.1 INTRODUCTION

In Chapter 3, we were concerned with the general problem of interpolation, viz., given the set of values $(x_0, y_0)$, $(x_1, y_1)$, ..., $(x_n, y_n)$ of $x$ and $y$, to find a polynomial $\phi(x)$ of the lowest degree such that $y(x)$ and $\phi(x)$ agree at the set of tabulated points. In the present chapter, we shall be concerned with the problems of numerical differentiation and integration. That is to say, given the set of values of $x$ and $y$, as above, we shall derive formulae to compute:

(i) $\frac{dy}{dx}, \frac{d^2y}{dx^2}, \dots$ for any value of $x$ in $[x_0, x_n]$, and

(ii) $\int_{x_0}^{x_n} y \, dx$.
