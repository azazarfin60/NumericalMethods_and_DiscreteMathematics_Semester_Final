- **Domain & Discretization:**
  - A 2D square domain discretized into a uniform $3 \times 3$ grid of 9 cells with equal grid spacing $h$.
  - Four interior node intersections are marked with circled numbers:
    - **Node 1:** Top-left interior grid intersection at $(x=1, y=2)$.
    - **Node 2:** Top-right interior grid intersection at $(x=2, y=2)$.
    - **Node 3:** Bottom-left interior grid intersection at $(x=1, y=1)$.
    - **Node 4:** Bottom-right interior grid intersection at $(x=2, y=1)$.
- **Boundary Conditions (Dirichlet):**
  - **Top Boundary ($y=3$):** Held at constant potential $V = 100\text{ V}$.
  - **Bottom Boundary ($y=0$):** Held at constant potential $V = 0\text{ V}$.
  - **Left Boundary ($x=0$):** Held at constant potential $V = 75\text{ V}$.
  - **Right Boundary ($x=3$):** Held at constant potential $V = 50\text{ V}$.
  - **Corners:** Diagonal slashes at all four corners indicate "Insulating Gap" to separate discontinuous boundary potentials.
